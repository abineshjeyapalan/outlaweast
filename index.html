Questions, answered.

The things people usually ask before they email us anyway.

An independent film studio based in Batticaloa, on the east coast of Sri Lanka. We develop, finance, and produce short and feature-length films, founded by an Academy Award–winning cinematographer and director.

Our studio and crew are based in Batticaloa. Most of our work is shot along the east coast, though we travel for the right story — our current feature has taken us to Kandy, Galle, and London.

Yes — always. We're looking for directors of photography, assistant directors, editors, sound designers, and production designers to join us, both on staff and per-project. Reach out on our Connect page.

Send a link to your reel, portfolio, or script (PDF, industry-standard format) through the form on our Connect page. We read everything that comes in, though it can take a few weeks given the size of the team.

Mostly 35mm and 16mm film, alongside digital when the schedule demands it. Our upcoming feature on Kumar Sangakkara is being shot entirely on IMAX 70mm.

We do both. We run a small crew traineeship out of Batticaloa each year for local talent, and we're open to co-production conversations with aligned studios — get in touch and tell us what you're building.
